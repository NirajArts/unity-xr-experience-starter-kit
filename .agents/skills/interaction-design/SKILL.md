---
name: interaction-design
description: Define XR action affordances, placement forgiveness, task completion, guidance modes and feedback for a training or exploration workflow.
---

Read Docs/INTERACTION_CONTRACTS.md and Docs/NEW_EXPERIENCE.md.

1. Express each action as observable input, mechanical result, feedback, completion and progression. Keep these separate.
2. Identify compatible parts, vacant destinations and permitted orientations. Choose tunable tolerances and visible nearby cues.
3. Preserve authored contacts and safe physical reach. Test novice interpretation: the intended tool should be identifiable before use.
4. Use actual action criteria, not arbitrary contact time or media completion. Do not invent engineering values.
5. Define guidance-off policy explicitly while preserving mechanical feedback, reset and completion.
6. Validate wrong/occupied placement, reverse approach, early release, drop and interruption. Confirm comfort/clarity on device when available.

Deliver implemented interactions and evidence, or a decision-ready proposal only when the user asks for design first.
